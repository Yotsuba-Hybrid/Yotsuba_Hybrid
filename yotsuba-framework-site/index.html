01 XNA lineage / modern GPU architecture

Yotsuba Framework. Next Gen XNA.

Yotsuba Framework is the only XNA-based framework to completely modernize its graphics architecture, incorporating Compute Shaders, Vulkan support on Android and an ecosystem that supports seven shader languages. It also enables shader compilation at runtime.

05 / SHADER SYSTEM

Six shader languages.
Plus direct SPIR-V.

Yotsuba Framework gives you multiple shader authoring paths instead of forcing one language everywhere. Use MGFX, FNAFX, Slang, native HLSL, GLSL or MSL — with direct SPIR-V support when that is the right input for your pipeline.
